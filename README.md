# ⚡ Electric Vehicle Market Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Microsoft Excel dashboard** designed to analyze electric vehicle (EV) market data, explore brand performance, and visualize key market indicators. This project uses data visualization, KPI cards, and interactive filters to present insights into the electric vehicle market.

## 📌 Project Overview

The Electric Vehicle Market Analysis Dashboard provides a visual overview of EV market performance across different brands, market segments, and countries.

Built using Microsoft Excel, the dashboard combines key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and interactive year filters to help users explore market trends and compare vehicle characteristics.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze average electric vehicle sales and pricing.
* Compare average prices across EV brands.
* Examine battery capacity distribution by brand.
* Explore sales distribution across different countries.
* Compare safety ratings across market segments.
* Identify key market indicators and brand performance.
* Present data through an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel**
* Pivot Tables
* Excel Charts
* KPI Cards
* Interactive Slicers and Filters
* Data Analysis and Visualization

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays important summary metrics:

* **Average Sales:** 1,296,653.6
* **Average Price:** 788,873.91
* **Average Customer Rating:** 3.6
* **Maximum Warranty:** 8 years
* **Highest-Selling Brand:** Tesla

*Note: These values reflect the dashboard screenshot and may change when the underlying dataset or filters are updated.*

### 2. Brand-Wise Average Price

A column chart compares average prices across electric vehicle brands, including Lucid, Porsche, Mercedes, BMW, Audi, Tesla, Rivian, Polestar, and other manufacturers.

**Purpose:** To understand price differences between brands and compare their market positioning.

### 3. Market Segment Analysis

A horizontal bar chart compares four EV market segments:

* Budget
* Mid-range
* Luxury
* Premium

**Purpose:** To visualize differences in sales or other measured values across market segments.

### 4. Safety Rating by Market Segment

A horizontal bar chart compares safety ratings across premium, mid-range, luxury, and budget vehicle segments.

**Purpose:** To explore differences in safety-related metrics between market categories.

### 5. Brand-Wise Battery Capacity

A doughnut chart visualizes the battery capacity distribution among selected electric vehicle brands, including Tesla, BYD, Volkswagen, Kia, and Hyundai.

**Purpose:** To compare the relative battery capacity values represented in the dashboard.

### 6. Country-Wise Sales

A doughnut chart displays the distribution of EV sales across countries, including China, Germany, Japan, South Korea, Sweden, and the United States.

**Purpose:** To examine the contribution of different countries to the sales values represented in the dataset.

### 7. Country-Wise Average Sales

A line chart presents country-wise average sales trends over the years 2020–2026.

**Purpose:** To visualize changes over time and identify potential sales trends.

### 8. Interactive Year Filter

The dashboard includes a year slicer that allows users to filter the displayed analysis by year.

**Purpose:** To make the dashboard more interactive and support year-based comparisons.
